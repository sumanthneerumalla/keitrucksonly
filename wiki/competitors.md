**Summary:** Two real listing competitors (Oiwa's KeiTruckUS, JDMBuySell); neither filters by the buyer's state, and Oiwa isn't neutral.

## Oiwa Garage / KeiTruckUS.com
- Parts retailer in Long Beach that also sells some trucks; KeiTruckUS lists 402 trucks from 18 dealers. (2026-10-31, handoff)
- Has a 50-state law map, price trends, 25-year-rule checker, landed-cost calculator; funnels to its parts store. (2026-10-31, handoff)
- Lacks: per-buyer-state registration filter, saved-search alerts, links out to dealers; not neutral. (2026-10-31, handoff)
- Owns FB group "Japanese Mini Trucks of America For Sale!" (56.3K, private) and charges dealers for >1 listing/week. (2026-10-31, facebook.md)
- r/keitruck has a pinned post "Reminder: Oiwa is an awful predatory company"; anti-Oiwa sentiment is an angle. (2026-10-31, facebook.md)
- Claims ~200k total Facebook group members. (2026-10-31, strategy)

## JDMBuySell
- Domain from 2005; ~1,000 kei listings; free listings with feed import; paid boosts $49 (3 d) / $149 (7 d) / $299 (14 d). (2026-10-31, handoff + strategy)
- Dominates programmatic SEO; uses Brevo and Postmark for email. (2026-10-31, handoff)
- Facebook page: 30,748 followers. (2026-10-31, facebook.md)

## Others
- Fourbie Exchange, JDM Supply, Mini Truck Depot, carmanji.com (dealer directory). (2026-10-31, dealer-ranking.md)
- Facebook Marketplace for private sales; since 2023 business pages can't post free vehicle listings. (2026-10-31, strategy)
