**Summary:** "kei truck" is the growing head term; "mini truck" is bigger but noisy. Peaks in December and April–June.

- Relative index, US. 5-yr avg: kei truck 8.5, mini truck 18.4, k truck 6.0, japanese mini truck 1.1. 12-mo avg: 13.5 / 32.9 / 9.7 / 1.2. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- "mini truck" is polluted by food trucks, toys, "nyt mini", "starlink mini"; had a non-kei spike Apr–Jun 2026. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- "kei truck" yearly avg 1.4 (2021) → 13.6 (2026 YTD), ~10x. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- Buyer intent: "kei truck for sale" vs "mini truck for sale" ≈ 0.85 ratio (was 3 vs 35 in 2021). (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- Seasonality (kei truck, 2022–2025): peak December, second peak Apr–Jun, low Sep–Oct, swing ~±25%. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- CONFLICT: strategy doc says searches peak in September; Trends data says September–October is the low. See [[open-questions]]. (2026-10-31)
- Top states by share, "kei truck": HI, WA, VT, AK, NH, UT, ID, MT, WY, CO, OR, AZ, ME, NC, NM. "kei truck for sale": HI, UT, WA, ID, VT, AK, CO, MT, NH. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- Wording: "kei" leads in the West and New England; "mini truck" ~60% share in the South/Midwest. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
- Rising queries: "are kei trucks legal in the us", "how much is a kei truck" +300%, "kei truck for sale <state/city>", comparisons with Slate/Telo/Maverick. (2026-10-31, trends.md)

## SEO implications
- "kei truck" as head term; "mini truck" / "japanese mini truck" as secondary wording. Pages: state/city for-sale, "are kei trucks legal in <state>", price guide, per-model pages. (2026-10-31, trends.md)
