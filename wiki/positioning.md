**Summary:** Free, neutral kei truck listings aggregator with a per-state "can I register it" filter. Revenue later from featured listings, never commissions.

## Pitch
- "Every kei truck for sale in the US, filtered to the ones you can actually register in your state." (2026-10-31, D-001)
- Edge over [[competitors]]: per-buyer-state registration filter, saved-search alerts, links out to dealers, neutrality. (2026-10-31, handoff)
- Domain keitrucksonly.com is owned; ktrucksonly.com is unregistered (buy + redirect). (2026-10-31, handoff)

## Timeline (playbook)
- Week 0 (Nov 2–8 2026): ktrucksonly.com, coming-soon page with email capture (email, state, model), email tool, social handles, dealer list.
- Weeks 1–4: build MVP ([[data-model]]) and email 40–60 dealers; target 15 dealers / ≥300 listings.
- Weeks 5–6: soft launch once ≥300 listings; r/keitruck mods first; first K Truck Price Index (Dec 2026). See [[distribution]].
- Weeks 7–12: weekly routine; private-seller listings open week 9.
- Decision point 2027-02-01: see [[kpis]].
- If continuing: paid featured listings (~$29–99) Feb–Mar 2027; Colorado push Apr–Jun 2027 before CO law takes effect 2027-07-01 (see [[state-laws]]).

## Background (strategy doc, directional only)
- The strategy doc argues for a broader "trust layer" (frame-number decoder, legality tracker, paid video diagnosis). v0 deliberately does listings + legality only. (2026-10-31, strategy)
- Market: ~1,800 imports in 2018, ~7,500 in 2023, >30k since 2020; median road-legal retail ~$8.8k. (2026-10-31, strategy)
- No dealer sells more than ~600/yr; market is very fragmented. (2026-10-31, dealer-ranking.md)
