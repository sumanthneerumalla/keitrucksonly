**Summary:** Facebook groups and r/keitruck. Owner posts personally (real name); message admins/mods first; unique post per group.

## Facebook groups (logged-out counts, 2026-10-31, facebook.md)
- Mini Truck Marketplace: 151.3K, public, 751 posts/mo, generic no-promo rule.
- Japanese Mini Trucks For Sale: 75.0K, public, dealers post freely.
- Kei Truck Owners: 58.3K, public; self-promo needs admin approval first.
- Japanese Mini Trucks of America For Sale!: 56.3K, private, **owned by Oiwa**, charges dealers. Avoid.
- Kei Truck Club: 52.5K, private, independent; "Promotions OK, but please don't spam". Best first post.
- KEI Trucks & Vans Inspiration FIND & LOCATE!: 31.6K, public.
- Dealer-owned (expect promo removal): Florida Kei Club 15.3K (Miyagi Garage), New England Mini Kei 14.6K (JDM Imports CT), TX/OK/LA/NM Buy and Sell 6.6K (Lonestar Mini Trucks).
- Regional: PNW Kei Community 4.7K (bans dealer ads and gen-AI content; pitch "free, no dealer fees"), NC KEI CLUB 725, Lone Star Kei 748 (Texas, bans cross-posting identical content).

## Reddit
- r/keitruck: 36,797 weekly visitors, 695 weekly contributions; self-promo only for vetted vendors with flair; buy/sell only in Saturday Swap Meet thread; low-karma accounts auto-blocked. (2026-10-31, facebook.md)
- Plan: aged account active ~1 week, apply for vendor flair, message mods before posting. (2026-10-31, facebook.md + playbook)
