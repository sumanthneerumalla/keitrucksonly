**Summary:** Go-to-market starts Mon 2026-11-02; the continue/rethink decision is Mon 2027-02-01. A KPI scorecard is drafted every Monday by the Digest + KPI agent.

## Thresholds (2027-02-01)
| Metric | Continue | Rethink |
|---|---|---|
| Live listings | >= 300 from >= 15 dealers | < 100 |
| Monthly visitors | >= 2,000 (winter) | < 400 |
| Clicks out to dealers / month | >= 150 | < 30 |
| Email subscribers | >= 500 | < 150 |
| Dealers confirming >= 1 sale from us | >= 3 | 0 |
(2026-10-31, playbook)

- The playbook says searches peak in September and run 30-40% lower off-season; Trends data disagrees. See [[search-demand]] and [[open-questions]]. (2026-10-31)

## If "continue"
- Feb-Mar 2027: paid featured listings for dealers (about $29-99) and a "Verified" badge. (playbook)
- Apr-Jun 2027: Colorado push ahead of its 2027-07-01 legalization (law page, dealers, saved-search campaign, local press). See [[state-laws]]. (playbook)
- Jul-Sep 2027: peak season; small ads test ($300-500) promoting the registerable search, not single vehicles. (playbook)

## If "rethink": find which number failed
- Listings low: supply problem; change the dealer offer.
- Visitors low: distribution problem. See [[distribution]].
- Clicks low: the product isn't useful; fix that before anything else.
(2026-10-31, playbook)

## Budget, first 90 days
- Under $50 plus owner time (domain ~$10, email tool $0, existing hosting, no ads until February). (2026-10-31, playbook)
