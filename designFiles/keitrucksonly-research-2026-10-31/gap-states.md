Checked 2026-10-31. Sources: JDM Supply state directories (rendered with Browser Farm because of a Vercel bot checkpoint), the carmanji.com/dealers directory, web search, and each dealer site checked directly (curl for homepage, products.json, WC Store API and sitemaps; RDAP for domain age; Browser Farm for JS-rendered pages). Rows that duplicate the 54-row prospect CSV are flagged. keitruckdirect.com is excluded.

## Why these states are thin

The gaps line up with state law (summaries from keitruckus.com/kei-truck-laws, Oct 2026):
- **GA, ME, OR:** no road registration. GA DOR refuses and voids kei titles, and HB 957 died in the Senate in 2026. Maine classes kei trucks as off-road vehicles that cannot be registered, and its 2026 working group recommended no change. Oregon DMV refuses them, and HB 4063 died in committee. Dealers in these states sell for off-highway or farm use only.
- **TN, OH:** registrable, but few dedicated lots.
- **CO:** HB25-1281 takes effect 2027-07-01. Road title and registration applications open 2028-01-01, for vehicles 25+ years old, on roads posted 55 mph or less. Until then sales go out on OHV/Parks and Wildlife registration.

Expect few dealers here. Every GA/ME/OR listing should carry an "off-road only in this state" note.
