# Kei Truck Dealer Sales-Volume Evidence (US + Canada)

Research date: 2026-10-31. Scope: **sales and import volume** and other size signals for the largest kei truck (Suzuki Carry, Daihatsu Hijet, Honda Acty, Subaru Sambar, Mitsubishi Minicab) dealers and importers. Current listing counts are covered elsewhere; they appear here only where they help identify a dealer or support a size estimate.

**Ground rules:** Nothing here is invented. "Self-claim" means the dealer said it about itself (website or marketplace profile). "Press quote" means the owner said it to a reporter. Rows marked **(est.)** are my calculations from stated inputs; the inputs are shown. Very few dealers publish unit sales, so treat the ranking as rough.

## Market context (for scale)

- About **7,500 kei trucks a year** are imported into the US. The figure is for 2023, from Japan Used Motor Vehicle Export Association (JUMVEA) data via NBC News. It was about 1,800 in 2018. https://www.nbcnews.com/business/autos/kei-trucks-are-gaining-popularity-us-small-size-low-prices-rcna156653
- About **30,000 kei vehicles** were imported over roughly five years (NHK World estimate, reported by The Drive on 2026-10-18). https://www.thedrive.com/news/japanese-tv-station-interviews-americans-about-their-love-for-kei-trucks-and-its-too-wholesome
- **What this means:** a dealer moving 300 a year has about 4% of the US market. The market is very fragmented. No source names a dealer that sells 1,000 or more a year, and no dealer claims a cumulative total of 2,000 or more trucks, except JDM Hawaii, whose figure covers all kei vehicles.

## Notes per dealer

- **JDM Hawaii:** This is the largest volume figure I found from a named owner. The roughly 2,000 total covers all kei vehicles, not only trucks, and serves only the Hawaii market (about 70% stays on Oʻahu). The basis is roughly 3.5 years, so about 570 a year. ImportKey shows only 14 shipments, which shows that bill-of-lading aggregators badly undercount roll-on/roll-off and broker-filed imports.
- **Boeki USA:** Founded 2017 per the marketplace profile. Its own site says 15 years of importing and "thousands of JDM cars" imported and sold. The 50–70 containers a year figure dates from 2019. Six trucks per container is the owner's own loading figure, but containers may also hold vans or cars, so 300–420 is an upper-range estimate. It has the most live listings of any dealer: 80 on keitruckus.com and 86–88 on jdmbuysell and its own site. It is the dealer behind the **NJ 80** count on keitruckus.com. Its Vancouver, WA site showed 0 in its own stock widget, and the **WA 38** count on keitruckus is The Import Guys, not Boeki. Panjiva shows 138 US shipments since 2007 (latest 2025-08-29), and ImportKey shows 35 shipments, all from Nipponway International. Both look low against the container claim, which suggests many imports are filed under a forwarder's or broker's name.
- **Skyline Kei Trucks:** "300+/yr" is a self-written marketplace profile ("Member since 2026"). I found no press confirmation. It had 20 listings on keitruckus.
- **Lancaster Mini Truck:** The 15–20 a month figure is recent (October 2026) and quoted by the owner. Retail prices are $6k–$8k. It is not one of the 18 dealers indexed on keitruckus.com.
- **Mayberry Mini Trucks:** It is behind most of the **NC 68** count on keitruckus (62 listings; Mars Mini Trucks has 6). It imports through Autocom Japan roll-on/roll-off ships into Jacksonville, FL. ImportKey's product counts (466 line items) cannot be safely read as vehicle counts, so no firm number is given.
- **HVNY Imports / Kei Truck King:** Both are in Goshen, NY. Kei Truck King says it was "featured in the NY Times." I could not confirm whether the two are related, or find the NYT article. The 300+ figure is from April 2023.
- **Duncan Imports:** It is behind the **VA 61** count on keitruckus. It is a general JDM and classic dealer, so kei trucks are only a share of its volume.
- **The Import Guys (Ferndale, WA):** It is behind the **WA 38** count on keitruckus. A full-time JDM importer since 2015; ImportKey shows Be Forward as a supplier. It sells mixed JDM vehicles and I found no kei volume figure.
- **Texas (TX 62 on keitruckus):** Weatherford Mini Trucks 26, Skyline 20, JDM Drive 10, Sunshine Imports 6. I found no sales figures or press coverage for Weatherford Mini Trucks.
- **Tiki Bunny and Krazy Kei:** Both are small Milwaukee operators with solid press figures (60 a year and 40 in 2025). They are useful as a benchmark for a typical small dealer.
- **Kei Trucks America (kei-trucks.com):** Quoted by NBC only on the national count ("tens of thousands" on US roads). Its jdmbuysell profile (West Lafayette, IN) has 4 listings and mentions wholesale and fleet sales. No volume figure.
- **Mini Truck Depot / Import Vehicle Services LLC:** A 2021 press release called it the "largest Japanese mini truck sales portal in the USA." That is a portal claim, not a sales figure. It is registered on SAM.gov. https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/getnews-2021-10-9-rebranded-minitruckdepotcom-launches-as-the-largest-japanese-mini-truck-sales-portal-in-the-usa
- **G&R Imports (Jackson, MO):** Calls itself the "longest-running" mini truck supplier in North America, a longevity claim rather than a size claim. 9 listings. No volume.
- **Canada:**
  - **Japanese Mini Trucks** (Regina/Saskatoon, SK) says its imports division began in 2005 and calls itself "currently the largest importer/reseller in Canada" (self-claim, japanesecartrade.com profile).
  - **West Shore Auto** (Highlands, BC; minitruck.ca) made an older "largest Canadian importer" forum claim. Panjiva shows 66 US-side shipment records, the latest from 2017.
  - **Samurai Trucks** (Grande Prairie, AB) claims to be "Canada's first JDM/kei importer, est. 2001."
  - **Keitruck.ca** (QC) has 10 listings.
  - None of these gives a volume figure.
